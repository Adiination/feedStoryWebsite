**Ye ek placeholder file hai.** Ise isliye rakha gaya hai taaki aap dekh sakein
ki layout, cover, reading time aur category page asli text ke saath kaise dikhte
hain. Poori file apni kahani se replace kar dein — sirf upar ka frontmatter block
rakhein aur uski values badal dein.

---

Saadhe das baje tak poora floor khaali ho gaya tha. Office mein wahi khaas
sannata tha — AC ki awaaz, aur koi baat nahi.

Meera ne ek ghante pehle hi spreadsheet dekhne ka natak band kar diya tha.

"Aapki train nikal jaayegi," Rohit ne kaha, screen se nazar hataaye bina.

"Aapki bhi."

"Main chaar stop door rehta hoon. Aap line ke aakhir mein." Usne apni chair ghumaayi.
"Risk barabar nahi hai."

Ye lagataar chautha Thursday tha. Dono ne is baare mein kuch nahi kaha tha, aur
ab wahi na kehna hi sab kuch keh raha tha — project Tuesday ko khatm ho sakta
tha, dono ko pata tha, aur har hafte koi ek wajah nikaal leta tha ki na ho.

Meera ne laptop band kiya. Bahar shehar apni lambi narangi dhundh mein tha, aakhri
train gyarah chaalis ki thi, aur abhi paune gyarah baje the.

"Theek se poochho," Meera ne kaha.

Rohit ne use ek pal zyada dekha — utna, jise nakaara na ja sake.

"Ruk jao," usne kaha.

---

*Aapki kahani yahan se aage badhti hai. Isi file mein likhein, save karein, page
refresh karein — aur kuch setup nahi chahiye.*
