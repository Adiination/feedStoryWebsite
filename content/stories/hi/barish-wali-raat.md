Mumbai mein barish faisla nahi poochti. Wo aati hai aur aapka poora plan uthaa kar
le jaati hai.

Aarav ki gaadi Worli ke paas hi ruk gayi — aage paani, peeche traffic, aur radio
par koi purana gaana jo dono ko pata tha lekin dono ne maana nahi.

"Do ghante," Naina ne phone dekh kar kaha. "Kam se kam."

"Ya poori raat."

Usne palat kar dekha. "Aap keh rahe hain ya umeed kar rahe hain?"

Aarav hansa, aur jawab nahi diya, jo apne aap mein ek jawab tha.

Saal bhar se yahi chal raha tha — office ki chai, ek jaise gaane, der raat ke
message jo dono subah tak padhte rehte the. Aur har baar koi wajah aa jaati thi:
kaam, gaadi, waqt, log.

Aaj raat koi wajah nahi bachi thi. Sirf barish thi, aur ek band sadak, aur do log
jo bahut samay se sirf sahi mauka dhoond rahe the.

Naina ne seat belt khol di.
