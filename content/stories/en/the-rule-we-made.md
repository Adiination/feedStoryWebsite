The rule was made on their fourth anniversary, in a hotel room in Goa, half
drunk, mostly as a joke: once a year, each of them got to ask for one thing, and
the other one wasn't allowed to be weird about it.

For seven years neither of them used it. That was the funny part. They'd bring it
up every anniversary, laugh, and let it pass — because the rule wasn't really
about asking, it was about knowing you could.

This year Dev put down his glass and said, "I'm using it."

Priya went still in a way that had nothing to do with alarm.

"Okay," she said.

"You don't know what it is yet."

"I know it's you." She turned her chair to face him properly. "Go on. I'm not
allowed to be weird about it."
